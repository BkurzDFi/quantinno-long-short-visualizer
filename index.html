What onboarding at Schwab looks like

If the client decides to move forward, here is a representative path for onboarding a DEALS Exchange account at Schwab: one firm-level setup, one portal setup, then a short per-account checklist before trading begins.

01

Firm-level custodial setup

One-time steps that establish the Quantinno relationship at Schwab.

  1. Assign Quantinno's manager account number (provided by Quantinno) to accounts.
  2. Enroll accounts in Schwab's Short-Interest Rebate Program (SIRP) - it offsets financing costs on the short book.
  3. Financing rates follow the firm's negotiated Schwab rate.
  4. Planning leverage above 145/45? Start firm-level Portfolio Margin approval with the Schwab RM early - it can take several days.
02

DEALS Portal setup

Quantinno customizes the portal for the firm before accounts onboard.

  1. Complete the Portal Setup Template: users, roles (Advisor, Client Service, Firm Admin), and team/pod access.
  2. Elect firm-wide custodians, DEALS strategies, and benchmarks so account onboarding stays consistent.
  3. Optionally set email CCs for transition analysis, onboarding, and service requests.
  4. The portal then handles Transition Analysis requests, account onboarding, service requests, and monthly statements.
03

Per-account checklist

Steps for each new DEALS account at Schwab before trading starts.

  1. Assign the firm's Quantinno manager account number, strategy name "Quantinno DEALS", and confirm SIRP enrollment.
  2. Change tax-lot disposition to HICO via the cost basis team.
  3. Enable margin; add Options Level 3 + Portfolio Margin application if leverage exceeds 145/45.
  4. Journal contributed securities from cash to margin (recently bought mutual funds wait 30 days).
  5. Set dividends to hold as cash, no reinvesting.
  6. Verify cost basis shows in Schwab Advisor Center - required for DEALS Exchange.
  7. Elect to grant proxies to Quantinno (their policy is not to vote them).
  8. Submit the New Account Onboarding form on the DEALS Portal.
Wash-sale monitoring

Linked accounts

Quantinno can watch other Schwab accounts for wash sales through a view-only SL (sub-link) master account: create the SL master, associate only the accounts to monitor, and sign a Data Access Agreement. Do not attach the SL master to the firm's main FA master account.

Timing tip

Submit margin paperwork early

Reg-T margin caps leverage at 145/45. Anything higher needs Portfolio Margin: $3M account minimum, 15% maintenance margin, Options Level 3, and firm-level Schwab approval that can take several days - submit as early as feasible so the account launch is not delayed.

Client caveats

Keep this visible

Loss generation is not guaranteed. Tax results depend on the client's facts, realization timing, wash-sale rules, state taxes, lot selection, and market behavior. This is an educational planning estimate, not tax or investment advice.
